# Errata policy

Preserve the published algorithm and make the smallest local correction that
states and proves its intended result. Verify each suspected issue against the
original PDF, including indentation, line wrapping and existing corrections.
Do not attribute extraction artifacts to the paper.

For each verified correction, record its exact location and before/after
excerpt in edits.json and the deterministic minimal.diff, provide a positive
Lean proof of the corrected operation, and immediately update the standalone
publishable TeX/PDF and Markdown evidence. Keep the errata compact for the
article and its dissertation reproduction. A corrected local label, offset or
mnemonic does not invalidate unrelated checked results.

The ideal unbounded RAM model remains the mathematical model; arbitrarily
large addresses can use finite variable-length operands. Bounded native x86
examples have their explicit realization contract. Continue the full proof
goal after each errata checkpoint, and retain the remaining literal-execution
and physical-refinement obligations in the ledger.
